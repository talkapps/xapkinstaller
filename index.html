Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how xAPK Installer handles information when you use our application.

1. Data Collection

xAPK Installer does not require you to create an account, and we do not track your personal activities or require any personal identification information to function.

However, we use a third-party service, Firebase Crashlytics, to collect crash reports and usage data. This helps us improve the stability and performance of our application. The data collected by Crashlytics includes information such as crash stack traces, device information including device type and operating system, and application usage data. This data is processed by Google.

You can learn more about how Firebase handles this data by reviewing Firebase’s privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

You can opt out of Crashlytics data collection through the settings in our application.

2. Permissions and Usage

To function correctly, the app requires access to your device's storage to find .apk and .xapk files, and the permission to install packages. These permissions are used exclusively for the app's core functionality: finding and installing the files you select.

3. Third-Party Apps

This app facilitates the installation of third-party software. Each of those applications has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any app you install.

4. Offline Functionality

The core features of xAPK Installer operate locally on your device. Apart from the crash reporting data mentioned above, we do not upload your files or data to any external servers.
