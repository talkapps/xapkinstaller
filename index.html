Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how xAPK Installer handles information when you use our application.

1. Data Collection

xAPK Installer does not require you to create an account, and we do not track your personal activities or require any personal identification information to function.

However, we use a third-party service, Firebase Crashlytics, to collect crash reports and usage data. This helps us improve the stability and performance of our application. The data collected includes crash stack traces, device information (such as device type and operating system), and general usage data. This data is processed by Google.

You can learn more by reviewing Firebase’s privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

You can opt out of Crashlytics data collection through the settings in our application.

2. Permissions and Usage

To function correctly, the app requires access to your device's storage to locate .apk and .xapk files, and permission to install packages. These permissions are used strictly for core functionality.

3. Third-Party Apps

This app facilitates installation of third-party software. Each app has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them before installation.

4. Offline Functionality

Core features operate locally on your device. Apart from crash reporting, no files or personal data are uploaded to external servers.
